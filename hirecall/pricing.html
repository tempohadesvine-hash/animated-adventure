How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer to tempohadesvine@gmail.com after we email your first booking and an invoice. Prices include no hidden fees; HST is added once HireCall is registered for it.

What if nothing gets booked?

Then you pay nothing for that sprint.

Can I stop early?

Yes. Email or call and we stop calling the same day. If a booking was already made, the sprint price applies.
