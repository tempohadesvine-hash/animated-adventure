How it works

Three steps from resume to interview

Step 1

Send your resume and target roles

Tell us the work you want — bartending, security, front desk, delivery and more — and when and where you can work.

Step 2

We call employers for you

A caller reaches the right manager during business hours, makes your case, and books an interview or trial shift.

Step 3

You show up and get hired

Every booking and callback lands in your email with names and times.

For employers

Got a call from HireCall?

An AI phone agent may call you, or answer when you call back, on behalf of one real candidate for one type of role. Ask it anything. Ask for an email follow-up to deal with the candidate directly, or ask to be taken off the list and we remove you right away.

Employers never pay for HireCall. To opt out, call (807) 699-8511 or email tempohadesvine@gmail.com.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is the caller a real person?

Calls are made by an AI phone agent that speaks for you, with a human (Tony) reviewing every booking. It always says it is calling on your behalf.

When do I pay?

Only after your first interview or trial shift is booked. If nothing is booked during your sprint, you owe nothing.

What areas do you cover?

Halifax, Dartmouth and Bedford to start. Ask if you are elsewhere in Nova Scotia.

Will employers know my number?

Only if you agree. By default, employers reach you through HireCall and get your contact details once an interview is booked.
