Privacy policy · effective October 14, 2026

Privacy

HireCall is operated by Tony Hinds in Halifax, Nova Scotia. This policy explains what we collect and why. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • From job seekers: name, email, phone, work area, target roles, availability, experience and resume.
  • From employers: business name, contact name, phone or email, and notes from calls (for example, interview times or a request not to be called again).
  • Call recordings and transcripts made by our phone system, kept to confirm bookings and improve call quality.

How we use it

  • To call employers on a job seeker's behalf and share their resume and contact details with employers they apply to.
  • To email bookings, callbacks and invoices.
  • To honour opt-out requests from employers.

We never sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use Retell AI (phone calls and transcripts), Netlify (website and forms) and Google (email). They process data for us under their own security terms and may store it outside Canada.

How long we keep it

Job seeker files are deleted 12 months after your last sprint, or sooner if you ask. Employer opt-outs are kept so we don't call again. Call recordings are deleted after 90 days.

Your rights

Ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing tempohadesvine@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
