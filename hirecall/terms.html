Terms

The service

HireCall calls employers on your behalf to request interviews or trial shifts for the roles you choose. We can't guarantee a job, an interview, or any wage.

Your part

  • Your information must be true. We won't call for roles you aren't legally able to do.
  • Show up to bookings or cancel with the employer directly and let us know.

Price and payment

Starter sprint $49 CAD; Full sprint $99 CAD. You are invoiced only after your first interview or trial shift is booked during the sprint, and pay by Interac e-Transfer within 7 days of the invoice. If nothing is booked, you owe nothing.

Calls

Calls are made to businesses during business hours by an AI phone agent that identifies itself as calling for you. Any employer who asks not to be called is removed immediately.

Ending

You can stop at any time by email or phone. We may decline or stop a sprint, for example if information is false.

Liability

Our total liability is limited to the amount you paid for the sprint. These terms are governed by the laws of Nova Scotia.
